## How to use this (each run)
- Pick the theme(s) with a live catalyst and MAP THE VALUE CHAIN — do not stop at the obvious large-cap name. The edge is in the under-covered links: suppliers, materials, enablers two layers down.
- Look for correlations and patterns across the market and in world events — what is happening geopolitically, macro-economically, and sector-wide that could move earnings power over the long run.
- Run promising names through ANALYSIS.md. Most days nothing clears the bar — that is fine.
- Not limited to large caps. Small/mid-caps and Canadian (TSX) names count, at any size — as long as they are real businesses with sourceable fundamentals.
- Log names you pass on (below) so you do not re-chew them, and so a pass can be revisited if the thesis changes.
- Current prices come from the LSEG snapshot (state/lseg-snapshot.md), refreshed each morning.

## Scope — what is IN and what is OUT
IN: quality businesses of any size — large, mid, or small cap; US or TSX; suppliers and enablers deep in a value chain — where a real, sourceable case can be made for mispricing against a long-term horizon.
OUT: pre-revenue or pre-resource speculation, penny stocks, junior miners/explorers, and any name whose thesis rests on drill results, binary regulatory events, or story alone rather than durable earnings power.
