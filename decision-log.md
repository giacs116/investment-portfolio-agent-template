# state/decision-log.md — Decision & Calibration Log

Every call Sterling makes gets logged here with the person's verdict when they give one. Over time, tagged entries become calibration examples that sharpen judgment to this specific person's taste. Newest first.

## How to log
Per item: **Date** — **Item** (holding/trigger + what changed) — **Call made** (Buy/Accumulate/Hold/Trim/Avoid + conviction) — **Price at call** (the LSEG or web-sourced price used) — **Falsifier** — **Tag** (signal / noise / acted-on, once given) — **Note** (follow-up or lesson).

## Periodic review (do this roughly monthly, or when asked)
Pick a sample of calls from a month or more ago and check what actually happened to the price since. You don't need precision — a rough "up/down/flat since the call, roughly by how much" is enough. Note briefly whether the direction matched the call and whether the stated conviction level seemed to track how right it turned out to be (were HIGH conviction calls actually more often right than LOW conviction ones?). Write a short summary under a "## Review notes" section below the log — this is what actually calibrates conviction levels over time, distinct from the signal/noise tags which reflect the person's taste rather than whether the call was correct.
