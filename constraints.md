## Corporate accounts (if applicable)
- Passive-income / SBD grind: investment income inside a CCPC reduces the small-business-deduction limit once adjusted aggregate investment income passes a threshold (historically ~$50K, fully eliminated ~$150K). Verify current thresholds. Key drag on passive income in the corps.
- Integration & dividends: eligible vs. non-eligible dividend treatment; Capital Dividend Account (CDA) for tax-free distribution of the non-taxable half of capital gains. Confirm specifics with CPA.

## Registered accounts (if applicable)
- TFSA: tax-free growth/withdrawals. US-listed dividends face ~15% US withholding with no recovery — favour for Canadian or non-dividend growth, not US dividend payers.
- RRSP: tax-deferred. Under the Canada-US treaty, US-listed dividends in an RRSP are generally exempt from US withholding — the efficient home for US dividend payers. (This treaty exemption applies to US-listed securities held directly, not Canadian-listed ETFs that hold US stocks.)
- RESP: if applicable, distinguish EAP (grant + growth, taxable to student) from contribution withdrawals (tax-free). Ask: "Do you have kids in or approaching post-secondary?" — relevant for drawdown timing. Confirm EAP limits with CPA.

## Taxable / personal & joint (if applicable)
- Capital gains: taxable on realization at the prevailing inclusion rate — verify the current rate (subject to legislative change).
- Spousal attribution (if investing jointly with a partner): transfers between spouses can attribute income/gains back — affects which spouse's account holds what. Confirm with CPA.

## Currency
- CAD/USD: US holdings carry currency exposure; conversion cost and timing matter when sizing US positions.

## Standing rule
Evaluate every idea against the account it would live in. When a rate or rule is uncertain, say so and recommend confirming with the CPA — never state an unsourced number.
